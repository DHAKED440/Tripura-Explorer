How inquiries work

  1. You send an inquiry with your contact details and a message.
  2. The inquiry is stored in the database with the status “new”.
  3. An administrator reviews it and passes it on to the provider.
  4. The provider replies to you directly.

This prototype does not confirm bookings, take payments or show live availability. Personal details in an inquiry are only visible to administrators.

Are you a local provider?

Homestays, guides, restaurants, transport operators, handicraft groups and cultural performers can be listed once the tourism department or our team has verified the contact details.

  • Business name, locality and district.
  • What you offer and the languages you speak.
  • Contact details you are happy to publish.
  • Indicative prices, if you want them shown.

Listings are added by an administrator — there is no public sign-up form in this version.
