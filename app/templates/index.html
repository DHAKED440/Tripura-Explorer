Plan your trip

Turn your interests into a day-wise plan

Choose the number of days, where you start and what you want to see. The planner matches published destinations to your interests, groups them by area and spreads them across your days.

  • 1. Pick your interests — nature, heritage, lakes, hills, temples, wildlife or culture.
  • 2. Review the suggested day-by-day stops.
  • 3. Sign in to save the trip, reorder stops and share a read-only link.

What the planner will and will not do

{{ icon("info", 18) }}Stops are grouped with straight-line distance when coordinates exist, otherwise by district.
{{ icon("alert", 18) }}Travel times, entry fees and opening hours are still being verified, so the plan is a suggestion — not an optimised route.
